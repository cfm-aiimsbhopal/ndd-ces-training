# NDD CES MP 2026: training materials

Training materials for the survey teams and nodal officers of the independent National Deworming Day Coverage Evaluation Survey in Madhya Pradesh (Khandwa, Chhindwara, Ratlam and Shahdol), led by the Department of Community and Family Medicine, AIIMS Bhopal, with NHM Madhya Pradesh and WHO.

The site holds the field manuals, the training deck, the List A/B household selection cards and a quick reference for field work. Team codes and the list of selected villages are not part of this repository.

Drafted with the assistance of Claude (Anthropic); reviewed by the AIIMS Bhopal survey team.
